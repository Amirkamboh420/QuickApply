     1→# QuickApply — Project Worklog
     2→
     3→## Project Overview
     4→QuickApply is the companion web dashboard for a job-application auto-fill Chrome extension.
     5→Built as a single-route Next.js 16 app with two modes: a marketing **Landing** site and a full
     6→**App Dashboard** (Profiles, Applications, Analytics, Cover Letters, Sites, Settings), switched
     7→via URL hash (`#app`). The product targets job seekers in Pakistan / South Asia and beyond,
     8→with local portal support (Rozee.pk, Mustakbil) as a key differentiator.
     9→
    10→Brand palette (user-specified): `#03045E` (ink), `#0077B6` (ocean), `#00B4D8` (sky),
    11→`#90E0EF` (shore), `#CAF0F8` (foam).
